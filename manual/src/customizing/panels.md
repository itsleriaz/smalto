# Panels and layout

## The panels

| Panel | Shows | Toggle |
|---|---|---|
| **Tools** | The tool rail | **Preferences → Panels** |
| **Layers and timeline** | The timeline | <kbd>Tab</kbd> |
| **Layers list** | The compact layers list (off by default) | <kbd>F8</kbd> |
| **Colors and palette** | The color picker and palette | <kbd>F6</kbd> |
| **Preview** | The animation preview | <kbd>F7</kbd> |

<kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Tab</kbd> hides every panel, including the tool options, so the canvas has the whole window; press it again to bring them back. A hidden panel leaves its room to the canvas.

## Two columns

The side panels (layers list, colors, preview) sit in two columns, one on each side of the window, and you can rearrange them:

- **Move a panel:** drag the grip at its top edge to the other column, or to another place in its column.
- **Tab panels together:** drop a panel onto another panel's title; they share the space as tabs.
- **Resize:** drag the gaps between panels, and their inner edges.
- **Reset:** **Preferences → Panels → Reset panel layout** puts them back where they started, at their usual sizes.

## Floating or docked

**Preferences → Panels → Layout → Style:**

- **Floating:** panels are cards over the canvas, with a soft shadow.
- **Docked:** panels fill the sides of the window, flush to its edges, so the whole sprite fits above the timeline.

## The tool rail

The tools are a rail on the left edge. For a short window, or by preference, it can run in several columns: drag the rail's right edge, or set **Tool columns** (1 to 4) in **Preferences → Panels**. A window too short for one column adds columns by itself.

## The timeline

The timeline is docked to the bottom. Drag its top edge to resize it. The **panel** button in its toolbar hides the layer column, leaving the room to the frames (handy when the classic layers list is on).

## Tool options

The strip under the tabs always shows the options of the current tool, so the canvas doesn't shift when you change tools. In a narrow window it scrolls sideways.
